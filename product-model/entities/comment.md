# Comment

A comment, or a reply to one, on a submission, character, journal or site update.

## Information kept

- **Content** — the comment's text
- **Posted at** — when it was posted
- **Reply to** — the comment it answers, if any
- **Hidden by** — who hid it

## States

### Visible

Shown under the item.

### Hidden

Hidden from everyone but staff, with its replies.

# Shout

A comment left on a member's profile, or a reply to one.

## Information kept

- **Content** — the shout's text
- **Posted at** — when it was posted
- **Reply to** — the shout it answers, if any
- **Hidden by** — who hid it

## States

### Visible

Shown on the profile's shouts.

### Hidden

Hidden from everyone but staff.
